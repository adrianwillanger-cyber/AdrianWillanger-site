Professional Evolution

From technology user to systems builder.

I've spent much of my career looking for practical ways to use technology to make work clearer, faster and more consistent.

Real estate provided the environment where much of that experience developed. It is a business where market conditions change quickly, information is often incomplete, and important decisions can carry meaningful financial consequences.

Over time, my use of technology expanded from digital marketing and real estate technology into data-driven market analysis, automated reporting, structured decision tools, AI-assisted workflows and reusable AI systems.

Better information isn't enough. It has to be organized into a system people can actually use.

That principle increasingly shapes the consulting work I do today.

The Foundation

Real estate is part of the story, not the limit.

I don't view my real estate career and my AI consulting work as separate paths.

Consulting Philosophy

I don't start with the technology. I start with the work.

Good technology should reduce complexity rather than introduce another layer of it.

Sometimes the answer involves AI. Sometimes it involves better data. Sometimes the biggest improvement comes from creating a clearer process.

The objective is not to use AI for its own sake. It is to build systems that make better work possible.

The Approach
  1. Understand the problem.
  2. Structure the information.
  3. Design the workflow.
  4. Apply the appropriate technology.
  5. Build in human judgment and review.
  6. Measure whether the result is more useful.

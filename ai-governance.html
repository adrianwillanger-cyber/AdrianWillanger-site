Why Governance Matters

AI adoption often moves faster than the rules around it.

Organizations need practical ways to document AI use, assign ownership, handle information responsibly, identify higher-risk decisions and decide where human review belongs. The goal is usable governance that supports the work rather than a policy that sits on a shelf.

Start with the work

Identify the tools, tasks, information, affected people and decisions that already involve AI or may involve AI soon.

Build proportional guardrails

Define practical boundaries, responsibilities and review points that reflect the organization’s actual uses and risk profile.
